# FurEver Faces policies (paste into Shopify: Settings → Policies)

## Refund policy

**Made just for you**
Every FurEver Faces item is made to order from your pet's photo, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind, a wrong size, or a photo you chose and later regret.

**You approve the art first**
Before anything is printed we email you a proof of your pet's portrait. Edits are free until you're happy. Nothing prints until you approve it.

**If something is wrong, we fix it**
If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or different from the proof you approved, contact us within 30 days of delivery through our Contact page with your order number and a photo of the problem. We'll send a free reprint or give you a full refund, your choice. You don't need to send the item back.

**Lost packages**
If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or your order is marked delivered and you can't find it, contact us and we'll reprint or refund it.

**Cancellations**
You can cancel for a full refund any time before you approve your proof. After approval the order goes to print and can't be cancelled.

**Refund timing**
Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

## Shipping policy

**Christmas delivery**
Order by December [DATE] for delivery before Christmas (US addresses). Orders placed after that date are still made and shipped, but may arrive after December 25. Please reply quickly to your proof email: your order prints only after you approve the art.

**How long it takes**
- Art proof: emailed within 1 to 2 business days of your order
- Printing: 2 to 5 business days after you approve the proof
- Shipping: 3 to 8 business days within the US

These are estimates, and carriers run slower in December.

**Shipping cost**
- Orders of $49 or more: free standard shipping
- Orders under $49: $5.99 standard shipping

**Where we ship**
We currently ship to US addresses. Everything is printed in the USA.

**Tracking**
You'll get a tracking email as soon as your order ships. Items in the same order may ship separately from different print facilities.

**Wrong address**
Please check your address at checkout. Contact us through our Contact page right away if it's wrong; we can change it until the order goes to print.
